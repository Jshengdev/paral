# cores

Promises: the logic as plain functions. Values in, values out. No files, no network, no clock, and no import of
settings: every setting arrives as an argument. So each one runs alone from a row.

| file | what it decides | in | out |
|---|---|---|---|
| `catch.ts` | his words, whether a line drafted early still fits them, and whether they ask for a drawing | key and ear events | words, final or not |
| `circle.ts` | what he drew around, and how his loop comes back | his path, the read lines, the display | line numbers; a ring, a box or his loop |
| `ground.ts` | where a quoted line is on his screen | a quote, the read lines, the display | one box in screen points, or not found |
| `happened.ts` | what this ask is, given the lesson | his words, the read lines, the last wrong line | asked, asked again, just tell me, changed and still wrong |
| `teach.ts` | the next rung | the rung and what happened | the rung to give, and whether to use new words |
| `push.ts` | whether a line may be shown: it may not hold his result as written, nor any number of it that is not on his own line (in digits or words, even as an example's outcome) | the line, the result, his line, a second look | show it, or a leak with the reason |
| `beats.ts` | what a hint or an explanation is made of, and whether a ring has room | the reply, the read lines | sentences, each with at most one mark; ring or box; what to wipe |
| `lesson.ts` | which example a clip may use; how long a clip runs | fixed examples, his page, his result | one example or none; seconds |
| `fit.ts` | whether a line the fast model wrote may be said aloud | the line | null, or why it is dropped |
| `hold.ts` | what the conversation has said and drawn | the lesson and one event | the new lesson |
| `partial.ts` | what can be trusted of a reply that is still arriving | the text so far | the finished fields and sentences, and the finished fields of the sentence being written |

Checked by: one row or more per file. Run one alone with `./check circle`.
