Milestone 2 is done

Everything below was checked through the browser and against the database.

What you should see

  1. The homepage has a one-line pitch with Sign up and Log in buttons.
  2. Logged out, opening the dashboard sends you to the login page.
  3. Sign up puts you on the dashboard, with your email and a Log out button.
  4. Paste a destination address, press Create link, and a short address appears in the list.
  5. Opening a short link records the click, then sends you to the destination.
  6. The stats page shows total clicks, 14 bars, top referrers and top countries.

Extra checks I ran

  • Passwords are saved scrambled.
  • A forged cookie is sent back to the login page.
